# Changelog

## v0.1.9 - 2026-11-24

- **Send data report** button at the top right of the helper: opens your data report, ready to copy (same as `/bog export`).
- Data reports can now go in the **#data-reports** channel on Discord. A bot ticks them with a check mark once they are in a release.

Recipe data from player reports:

- Enchant Bracer - Lesser Intellect: taught by Gimble Thistlefuzz (Alliance)

## v0.1.8 - 2026-11-23

- New Quests tab: profession quests for each profession you have, with Done / In quest log / Not done, what the
  quest asks for, and a map pin on the quest giver.
- Recipes tab: "Not learned only" tick box.
- Vendors and trainers that stand somewhere else than the data says are noticed; your hover and map pin use where
  you saw them, and the move goes into your data report.
- NPCs that walk a route (like Zixil) have their route drawn on the world map.
- Recipe data from player report #4.

## v0.1.7 - 2026-11-22

- The map pin goes to your own faction's trainers and vendors, and only to the other faction's when they are the
  only source.

## v0.1.6 - 2026-11-22

- AH Sell tab removed for now.

## v0.1.5 - 2026-11-22

- Recipes search also matches materials, stats and effects of what a recipe makes, and enchants.
- Shift-click a recipe or material to link it in chat.
- Recipes hover shows faction, zone, continent, coordinates, distance and direction.
- Recipe data from the latest cMaNGOS world database.

## v0.1.4 - 2026-11-20

- Recipe data from player report #2 (Merchant's Favor vendors, Stephen Ryback).

## v0.1.3 - 2026-11-20

- The window title shows the addon version.
- Money Maker: one tick box per profession to show or hide its recipes.
- Recipe data from the first player report (Reagent Bag patterns sold in Ironforge).

## v0.1.2 - 2026-11-20

- Search box on the Recipes tab.
